# Week 4 – Data Visualization & Storytelling

## Objective

The objective of this project was to transform business data into meaningful visualizations using Microsoft Excel. Multiple chart types were used to identify trends, compare categories, analyze relationships, and communicate insights effectively.

## Visualizations Created

1. Revenue by Product (Horizontal Bar Chart)
2. Orders by Payment Method (Column Chart)
3. Monthly Revenue Trend (Line Chart)
4. Unit Price vs Total Price (Scatter Plot)
5. Order Status by Payment Method (Stacked Bar Chart)
6. Referral Source Distribution (Pie Chart)

## Key Findings

- Chair products generated the highest revenue.
- Online payments were the most frequently used payment method.
- Revenue fluctuated throughout the observed period.
- Higher unit prices generally resulted in larger transaction values.
- Most orders were processed successfully.
- Referral sources contributed differently to customer acquisition.
